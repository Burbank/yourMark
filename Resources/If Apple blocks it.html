If Apple blocks the app

This is a help page, not a program. Apple has not notarized yourMark yet, so macOS may say it “could not verify” the app. That is Gatekeeper — not malware.

Open Privacy & Security Settings

Safari may ask to open System Settings. Click Allow. Then scroll to Security and press Open Anyway.

1. On the “Not Opened” dialog, click Done — not Move to Bin.

  1. Click the blue button above, or: Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll to Security (near the bottom of that pane).
  3. Next to “yourMark.app” was blocked to protect your Mac, click Open Anyway.
  4. Confirm Open Anyway on the next dialog, then open yourMark from Applications.

You can also right-click yourMark → Open.
