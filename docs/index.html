Help

        
      

      

        
This tab
A browser preview. Drop a PDF with selectable text. Nothing is uploaded. The browser version does not include OCR.

        
Mac app
Better for PDFs with graphics. OCR and layout. Scans, tables, and figures stay intact there.

        
Convert
Drop a PDF or Word file. Repeating headers can be dropped in Settings. Numbered titles become headings.

        
Library
Cards on the left. Bookmarks in the middle — the PDF outline when it has one. Click to jump.

        
Ask
Answers come from the open chapter, in this tab. A real model needs the Mac app; browsers often block the key.

        
Edit
Download the Markdown. Mac: MarkEdit. Windows: MarkText. Both are free and open source.

        
Picture links
On the Mac app, pictures are blue links. Hover for a small preview. Click to show that file in Finder. The browser preview does not include OCR or those links.
